Kan Live Clips

Kan Live Clips is a personal tool that finds the most exciting moments in live gaming streams (Twitch and Kick), cuts them into short vertical videos with credit to the streamer, and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

It is used only by its owner to publish to their own accounts. It does not offer sign-up, does not sell anything and does not collect data from the public.
